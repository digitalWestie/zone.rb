
## THE CROSSING

The outer checkpoint, a rather substantial building encased in
what looks like lead. A place, so far you've only seen from the outside.
You've been here before, in the many field notes you've read.

Inside, you see the fabled railway track. Guards in gray, and beyond them,
a gate large enough for freight, sealed.

Your colleague is already here. Although they have an Institute field
kit, it doesn't look weighed down by any sensory equipment.

COLLEAGUE
  You're the volunteer, let me introduce myself, I'm Cay. They told me
  your readings-expertise are to be useful.

  Have you ever been inside? No, you look a little young for-

They tip their head toward a figure standing on the platform.

---

COLLEAGUE
  And there is our contracted expertise, our Stalker.

A guard shows him over to you.

GUIDE
  I'm here to guide you through- to help you see what you need to. For
  your safety, you listen to me and you do as I say.

  Call me Tunnock.

A guard checks you and Cay's papers. Nobody checks the guide's;
the plea arrangement is already a stamp in a book somewhere else.

Radios murmur. The gate answers with a deep mechanical breath.
Bolts withdraw.

---

CAY

  Hear that Stalker? They're opening the vault for you...

The guide does not answer. He steps onto the motorized rail trolley,
taking the forward seat. In that moment, his face shows an absence.
His mind is already somewhere else.

Cay's smile thins, and he steps onto the trolley.

COLLEAGUE
  Right. Then.

You sit. The trolley takes the rail with a shudder. You feel
the ground beneath you shake as you leave the checkpoint behind you.
