
# Prologue

## Dr. Varga's Office

For months, you've imagined this meeting. The image disturbed your
furious study of field notes and readings. But thankfully, you're here.

Blinds half-drawn. Coffee and toner in the corridor leak beneath
the door.

A man stands by the bookcase at the end of the office. His visitor
badge is signed in minuscule handwriting. His outfit a little too
sharp for the Institute. He does not introduce himself.

VARGA
  I hope this doesn't disappoint.

  This expedition is not the first sanctioned here since the policy
  change. Nor is it to gather the samples you expected.

Every version of the meeting you imagined has just been discarded.

---

VARGA
  Five days ago, a team of three entered the site. Their task was
  to take readings in areas we identified as stable. No samples. No
  artefacts.

  They have not returned.

VARGA
  Your task will be to join a recovery team, and bring back anything
  related to their expedition.

  You will be accompanied by one of our own, and a contracted guide. He
  has a plea arrangement for his trespassing. We admit he has ventured
  further than any of our policies have allowed.

VARGA
  I trust you are aware of the sensitive nature of this work.

---

VARGA
  You will carry sealed orders. These remain unopened until the
  condition on each envelope is met.

The visitor stands, sets a packet of envelopes on the desk without
comment.

VARGA
  When you are back, and you will be back, we can look properly at
  your work... "Alternative Intelligence and Re-interpreting the
  Visitation"?

  Please sign the following documents, and make your preparations.

You sign.
